# Greenhouse ground materials

The three material sets (`brown_mud`, `concrete_floor_01`, and `sparse_grass`) are 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/). They are distributed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Asset pages:

- https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud
- https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_01
- https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass

They are bundled locally. The greenhouse structure, equipment, crops, and controls remain project-authored procedural geometry; the material maps are not a scanned or site-calibrated greenhouse model.
